# The case that proves the demand pager is not merely busy, but correct:
# processes must page in, page out, AND still run to completion. A pager that
# livelocked - evicting the very page the faulting process just brought in -
# would show plenty of paging here and zero finished processes.
initialize
scheduler-start
@sleep 5
scheduler-stop
@sleep 2
screen -ls
vmstat
exit
